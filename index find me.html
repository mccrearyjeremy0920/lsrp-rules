01Read This First

These rules apply from the moment you connect to the city, the Discord or any LSRP stream, clip or social post. Not knowing a rule is not a defense. Rule numbers are fixed so staff and players can cite them ("Rule 4.3").

What Lakeshore is. A semi-serious roleplay city. Characters are people with fears, histories and bills. Crime, police and medical are all real jobs with real consequences. We want stories, not stat-chasing.

1.1 Rule that closes every loophole

1.1.1 Intent over wording. If you do something that has the same effect as a banned action, it is banned. "The rule didn't say that exact thing" is never a defense.

1.1.2 Staff decide edge cases. If a situation is not covered, staff rule on it using the spirit of Section 1.2. The ruling then becomes a written rule within 7 days.

1.1.3 Comply now, appeal after. If staff give you an instruction in the city, follow it immediately. Argue it afterward through a report (Rule 9.4). Arguing in the moment is its own violation.

1.1.4 You are responsible for your group. Gang leaders, crew organizers and business owners answer for rule breaks they order, allow or hide (Rule 5.2.4).

1.1.5 Rules stack. Breaking two rules in one act is two violations.

1.1.6 No penalty for an unwritten rule the first time. If staff rule on something this book does not cover (Rule 1.1.2), the first offense is a warning only, and the rule is written down within 7 days. Rule 1.1.1 still applies when the act clearly has the same effect as a banned one.

1.2 The spirit of the server

  1. Make the other player's scene better, not shorter.
  2. Give people time to react (Rule 3.4).
  3. Risk must be real for everyone, including you.
  4. Win by story, not by exploiting.
  5. Outside the city, be decent.

1.4 Before you play

1.4.1 You must have a working microphone and use voice chat for roleplay. Typing is for /me, /do and commands only.

1.4.2 One account per person. No shared accounts.

1.4.3 LSRP is 18+. Act like an adult: no kid behavior, no tantrums in the city or the Discord.

1.4.4 English is the server language in public. Use it in the city and Discord support channels.

1.4.5 You must read this book once. The first time you join you will be asked to confirm that you did.

02Community Conduct (City, Discord, Streams)

2.1 Respect

2.1.1 No harassment, threats, doxxing, stalking, or targeting a person over time, in or out of the city.

2.1.2 No hate speech, slurs or discrimination about race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexuality, disability or nationality. This applies IC and OOC. A character may be rude or criminal. A character may not be a vehicle for real-world hate. "It was my character" is not a defense.

2.1.3 No sexual content involving minors, ever. No non-consensual sexual roleplay. Sexual roleplay is not allowed in public spaces, and is limited to private, mutually agreed scenes between adults with a fade-to-black. Staff may end any scene.

2.1.4 No real-world self-harm or suicide roleplay or talk used to guilt, threaten or pressure anyone. If a player is in real distress, stop the scene and tell staff.

2.1.5 Keep real politics and real tragedies out of the city.

2.2 Privacy

2.2.1 Never share another player's real name, address, workplace, photos, voice, or account details.

2.2.2 Do not record or share private voice calls from outside the city. Do not post staff tickets, staff chat or Discord DMs without permission from every person in them.

2.3 Honesty

2.3.1 No lying to staff. Lying in a report, ticket or appeal doubles the penalty on the original issue.

2.3.2 Do not edit clips. A clip must show the full event from at least 60 seconds before it started to when it ended.

2.4 OOC speech

2.4.1 OOC talk in the city is allowed for technical issues, emergencies and staff matters only. Use /ooc, and keep it to one short line.

2.4.2 You may not give or ask for in-city information through Discord, text or a stream during an active scene (see Rule 3.3).

2.4.3 Do not use OOC to avoid a consequence: no "wait, I have to go" in the middle of a scene to dodge it (Rule 3.7).

2.5 Streaming and content creation

2.5.1 You may stream and record. You must delay your stream by at least 2 minutes, with your location and voice chat visible only on the delay.

2.5.2 Do not show other players' streams, Discord or private chat in the city while you are IC.

2.5.3 Stream sniping is a violation of Rule 3.3 and gets the harsher penalty in Section 9.

2.5.4 Staff may ask you to stop or change a stream. Comply immediately (Rule 1.1.3).

2.6 Advertising and poaching

2.6.1 No advertising other servers, communities or sales, in the city, Discord or DMs.

2.6.2 No recruiting our staff or players away.

2.7 Accounts

2.7.1 Alts may not benefit your main in any way: no moving money, items, vehicles or information between them. Both accounts are penalized.

2.7.2 Ban evasion with a new account gets all linked accounts banned permanently.

03Core Roleplay Rules

3.1 Fail RP

3.1.1 Definition. Fail RP is acting in a way that is not believable for your character or the situation, or that breaks the story for others.

3.1.2 Examples that are always Fail RP: walking away from a crash that should have hurt you; sprinting normally with a serious injury; ignoring a gun pointed at you; driving normally after being told to stop by police and then stopping only because the pursuit became inconvenient; "forgetting" something to avoid a consequence.

3.1.3 Your character reacts to injuries, fear and pain. The injury system sets the minimum. You may act worse than the system, never better.

3.1.4 Your character has a job, a home and a life. A character who commits only crimes and has no other visible life for more than 14 days may be asked by staff to explain their income story.

3.2 Your character

3.2.1 Your character must have a real-sounding first and last name. No celebrities, no real criminals, no jokes, no names taken from fiction.

3.2.2 You may have up to the character slots your account has. Characters on the same account may not know each other IC, may not help each other, and may not trade between them (Rule 2.7.1).

3.2.3 Do not change your character's face, voice or name to avoid police or a consequence. Appearance changes need a believable in-story reason and must go through the in-game clothing and barber systems.

3.2.4 Your character does not know what you know. Your character has only learned things they saw, heard or were told in character.

3.3 Metagaming

3.3.1 Definition. Metagaming is using information your character has not learned in character to make a decision.

3.3.2 This includes: using Discord, streams, clips, text, voice calls or a second screen to learn where someone is or what they are doing; using a name from the scoreboard, nameplates or a dead player's identity; using the knowledge you have from another of your characters.

3.3.3 You may act on information only if your character received it IC (seen, heard, told, found, read in an in-game item).

3.3.4 Telling others in Discord to "come to the city" during an active scene is metagaming. Their characters may not respond to it.

3.3.5 Stream sniping is watching or listening to another player's stream to gain an advantage. Penalty: Section 9, Tier 3.

3.4 Powergaming

3.4.1 Definition. Powergaming is forcing an outcome on another player, or giving your character abilities, items or immunity that the game does not give.

3.4.2 You must give the other player time to react: /me actions are attempts, not results. "/me tackles him to the ground" is wrong. "/me tries to tackle him" is right.

3.4.3 No /me that does something the game has a mechanic for. Use the mechanic (cuff, search, carry, stash). Do not roleplay around it.

3.4.4 No stating that you hold an item you do not have in your inventory. No describing injuries on someone else.

3.4.5 If the other player cannot respond (they are unconscious or in a menu), you may only do what the game allows.

3.5 Death, downed and New Life Rule (NLR)

3.5.1 When you are downed and revived, you remember what happened to you. You may name your attacker only if you saw them IC.

3.5.2 When you respawn after death (not revived), you forget the circumstances that led to your death. You may not go back to the area of death for 20 minutes. You may not use what you know against the people who killed you.

3.5.3 You may not take revenge for a respawn death. You may take revenge for being downed and revived, after the 30-minute wait in Rule 4.4.2.

3.5.4 Your character may not talk about what happened to a respawned character with anyone IC.

3.6 Value of life (NVL)

3.6.1 Definition. NVL is not acting like you want to live.

3.6.2 With a gun on you from a clear threat, you must comply, run in a believable way or negotiate. You may not run toward the shooter, draw on a group with a bigger force, or pick a fight you cannot win.

3.6.3 You may not use your life as a weapon: no jumping off buildings, suicide-by-police, driving into a crowd, or blowing yourself up to gain an advantage.

3.6.4 Self-sacrifice is allowed only with a clear IC reason that you have roleplayed first and that staff would call a believable story beat. It is rare and cannot be farmed for gain.

3.7 Disconnecting and combat logging

3.7.1 You may not disconnect, alt-F4 or crash to avoid a scene, an arrest, a search, a death or a hostage situation.

3.7.2 If you drop for a real reason, tell staff in Discord within 10 minutes. Reconnect and rejoin the scene as it was if it is still running. If it has ended, you accept the result.

3.7.3 A disconnect during a scene counts as consent to a search of your belongings and a seizure of what you carried, as if you were still there.

3.7.4 Logging off inside a hideout, home or apartment is allowed only when you are not in a scene.

3.8 Communications

3.8.1 Do not use voice-changers that alter pitch to pretend to be another gender or age. Light changes for character voice are allowed.

3.8.2 In-game radio, phone calls and texts are IC. A call is overheard if someone is in range.

3.8.3 Discord is never IC unless an event says it is.

3.9 /me, /do and the world

3.9.1 /me describes what you do. /do describes what can be seen or sensed. Neither changes game state, and neither can be used to do a thing the game has no mechanic for (for example, "/do the door is unlocked").

3.9.2 Respect the map: no entering closed interiors, no using props as ladders, no hiding in objects or the water to avoid a scene.

3.10 Character kill (CK)

3.10.1 A CK is the permanent end of a character. It requires both (a) staff approval and (b) the agreement of the player whose character is ending, or a staff ruling for rule breaks.

3.10.2 Nobody can CK another character without staff approval. Threatening a CK is an RP tool, but carrying it out is a staff decision.

3.10.3 After a CK the character's name, face and possessions (except what staff allow) are retired. The player may make a new character at once; the new character does not know the old one's life.

04Violence and Conflict

4.1 Starting violence

4.1.1 Valid reason. You may start violence only with a valid reason that your character has and can explain in under 30 seconds to staff. A valid reason is one of: (a) self-defense against an immediate threat, (b) a declared gang war (Rule 5.6), (c) a robbery, kidnapping or heist you are committing under Section 6, (d) a debt or insult that has been escalated in roleplay for at least 5 minutes with a clear warning, (e) an event or scene staff have approved.

4.1.2 Random Death Match (RDM). Killing or attacking a player without a valid reason. RDM includes shooting someone because they walked into your area, because you were bored, or because "they looked like a cop."

4.1.3 Vehicle Death Match (VDM). Hitting a player with a vehicle on purpose without a valid reason. Ramming is allowed only in a pursuit (Rule 8.3) or as part of a valid reason.

4.1.4 No kill on sight. There is no kill-on-sight. The only exception is two gangs that are in an active war (Rule 5.6), and only with each other's members.

4.1.5 Warn first. Before using lethal force on a person who is not already attacking you, you must give a clear verbal warning and 10 seconds to comply. Exception: someone already shooting at you or at another person.

4.1.6 Do not shoot unarmed or surrendering people (hands up, kneeling, cuffed, ziptied or in a menu). A surrender stands until they attack.

4.2 Size of fight

4.2.1 Fights between criminals are limited to 6 people per side. Over that, you must retreat, surrender or ask staff for an event.

4.2.2 You may not bring more than twice the number of players than the other side has to a fight unless they started it.

4.2.3 Gang war rules (Rule 5.6) replace 4.2.1 for declared wars, with a cap of 8 per side.

4.3 Escalation

4.3.1 Use the least force that matches the threat. Fists with fists, knives with knives, guns with guns. If you pull a gun first on an unarmed person, you need a stronger valid reason.

4.3.2 Do not use explosives, grenades or fire against a person except in a declared war, a heist (Rule 6.3) or an approved event.

4.3.3 Drive-bys are allowed only in a declared war or as part of a valid reason. A drive-by in a safe zone or in a public crowd is prohibited.

4.4 After the fight

4.4.1 Looting the dead. You may take weapons and illegal items only. Wallets, phones and personal items require the victim's consent or a valid robbery.

4.4.2 Revenge. You may not hunt the person who downed you (and was revived) for 30 minutes. You may take revenge on a different scene through proper RP.

4.4.3 Cool-down. After a fight where someone died, the attackers and defenders may not start another fight with the same group for 30 minutes unless the other group starts it.

4.4.4 No chasing. You may not chase a person across the map to kill them after they have escaped. The scene ends 3 minutes after the last contact.

4.5 Kidnapping and torture

4.5.1 A kidnapping needs a valid reason and may last up to 20 minutes IC before a decision (release, ransom or end). The kidnapped person may be held no more than 45 minutes in total without staff approval.

4.5.2 Torture and abuse roleplay must stay inside what the other player is comfortable with. Any player may say "stop RP" and you must stop. Graphic content is limited to what the game's animations show.

4.5.3 You may not kidnap a person who is in the middle of a job, a medical scene, an arrest, or who has just spawned.

4.5.4 You may not CK a kidnapped person. See Rule 3.10.

4.6 Protected people

4.6.1 Players doing EMS roleplay may not be attacked or robbed while treating a patient, except in a declared gang war when they are treating a member of the other gang and they were warned. (Specific EMS duty rules are in the EMS handbook.)

4.6.2 Players in the first 30 minutes of a new character may not be robbed or kidnapped. They may be recruited or scammed through roleplay.

05Criminal World Rules

These rules cover the Burner Tablet, drugs, gangs, turf, guns, laundering, burglary, heists and the grey market. Section 4 (violence) and Section 6 (robbery and hostages) apply on top of them.

5.1 The Burner Tablet

5.1.1 One tablet per character. Progress belongs to the character. You may not sell, trade, rent or lend your progress, your login or your tablet's contacts.

5.1.2 Characters with a police job may not buy or use a tablet. If a character switches to a police job, their tablet use stops (the progress is kept).

5.1.3 A seized tablet shows only a limited view. Do not try to read the owner's progress OOC from a seized tablet. Police learn only what the MDT shows.

5.1.4 Freelancers (Driver, Fixer, Fence, Lookout, Gunsmith) set their own prices inside the allowed range. Setting a price outside the range, hiring yourself or an alt, or trading catalog ratings is a violation (Rule 2.7.1).

5.1.5 Catalog jobs are paid through escrow. You may not take payment outside escrow and then abandon the job, or ask for a refund after completion.

5.2 Gangs

5.2.1 Only staff-approved groups are gangs. You may not call yourselves a gang, wear gang colors, claim turf or hold a hideout until staff approve you in the gang tool. Applications go through staff only.

5.2.2 Ranks are Boss, Lieutenant, Member and Prospect. Staff set the Boss. The Boss may promote and demote inside the gang tool.

5.2.3 A gang that has no active member for 14 days is flagged. Staff may suspend or disband a flagged gang.

5.2.4 Leaders are responsible. A Boss or Lieutenant who orders, allows or covers up a rule break by a member shares the penalty. Gang-wide strikes are possible for repeated violations (Section 9).

5.2.5 Joining and leaving. After leaving or being removed from a gang, a character must wait 3 days before joining another gang. This does not apply if staff removed the gang. Freelancers listed in the catalog are paused while in a gang.

5.2.6 Gang hopping. You may not join a gang only to take its turf, stash or contacts and then leave. Joining must be a story, not a tool.

5.2.7 Gang colors. Members must wear their gang's colors during a war, an organized fight or a heist so police and opponents can identify them. Colors are set at approval.

5.2.8 Alliances. Gangs may trade or work together. They may not ally in a war against the same enemy (no "I'm in your gang for this fight only"). Joint heists follow Rule 6.2.

5.2.9 Betrayal (insiding). A member who passes information or access to another group is allowed IC only if: the character has been in the gang for at least 3 days; the information was learned IC; and no item or cash has been removed outside the stash system. You may not join a gang with a plan to betray it from the first day.

5.2.10 Hideouts are safe from robbery. Nobody may enter a hideout who is not a member or invited by a member. No hideout can be robbed. Police may raid one only with an MDT case and a warrant. A member who invites an enemy to rob a hideout is breaking Rule 5.2.9.

5.2.11 Member cap. A gang may have at most 15 members on its roster (Boss, Lieutenants, Members and Prospects all count). Staff can raise the cap for one gang in writing. Splitting a gang into a second approved gang to get around the cap is a Tier 3 violation.

5.2.12 New-player wait. A character cannot join a gang until the player has at least 3 days of total playtime on the server. This applies to the player, not the character, so a new character on an old account is not held back.

5.2.13 No recruiting during a war. A member who joins a gang while it is in an active war may not take part in that war.

5.3 Dirty cash and laundering

5.3.1 Dirty cash is a physical item. You may spend it only on supplies (including guns and parts from the arms dealer), bribes and gang dues. It cannot buy anything else.

5.3.2 Dirty cash is not a payment for services between players, rent, vehicles, houses or business purchases. If you pay for those with dirty cash through a loophole, both sides are penalized.

5.3.3 You may not convert dirty cash to clean cash by any method other than the laundromat, an accepted business deposit that the owner has switched on, or an approved script. Moving dirty money through a friend's bank account, or selling an item at a loss to a friend to "wash" the money, is a violation.

5.3.4 A business owner who accepts dirty deposits does so voluntarily and takes the audit risk. You may not force a business owner to accept dirty deposits. A business owner may not accept dirty deposits and refuse police audit access.

5.3.5 Never buy or sell in-game money, items or characters for real money, outside the server's official store.

5.4 Drugs

5.4.1 You may sell drugs to NPC buyers anywhere except safe zones (Rule 8.4). You may not sell near a school or hospital building.

5.4.2 Selling to a player is allowed only through RP. A player may refuse. You may not force-sell to a player.

5.4.3 No macros, auto-clickers, scripts or bots. Only you, with your own hands, may do the selling and cooking.

5.4.4 No AFK farming. You must be at your keyboard for any action that makes money. Leaving a plot, lab or sale unattended for more than 10 minutes counts as AFK.

5.4.5 A batch's quality, price, batch number and demand are set by the server. You may not exploit a price mistake. If you find one, report it (Rule 7.3).

5.4.6 Police may use drug evidence from a lead only through the MDT. You may not lie to staff about drug ownership to protect someone.

5.5 Turf and tags

5.5.1 Turf blocks are held through influence the server counts (sales, tags, rackets, contracts). Holding a block does not give you the right to kill anyone on it (Rule 4.1.1).

5.5.2 Tags. Spraying over another gang's tag is a valid IC provocation. It is not a valid reason for violence by itself. The war rules (Rule 5.6) decide what comes next.

5.5.3 Protection deals are opt-in on the tablet. You may threaten in roleplay, but you may not take money from a business, damage it, or hurt its staff to force a deal outside the tablet system. Taking from a register is a robbery (Section 6).

5.5.4 Anchor pots. A pot may only be collected by the gang holding the block, or by a robbery that follows Section 6. You may not camp an anchor all day to deny a rival's collection (see Rule 5.5.5).

5.5.5 No camping. Standing at a contested location for longer than 30 minutes without a scene or an active war counts as camping. Staff may order you to leave.

5.6 Gang wars

5.6.1 A war is declared on the tablet by a Boss or Lieutenant. It starts after the notice time shown on the tablet (default 30 minutes). The defender may show up or concede. Conceding ends the war and gives the other gang the turf result.

5.6.2 A war is only between the two gangs named. Both sides' members are KOS to each other, only while the war is active and only for the people who wear gang colors (Rule 5.2.7).

5.6.3 Maximum 8 members per side at one time. Reinforcements may replace members who left or died but may not exceed 8.

5.6.4 Kills alone do not score. Holding the block, tags and anchor control do. You may not farm kills to reach a score.

5.6.5 A war ends at the next restart, when one side concedes or is wiped out, or when staff end it. After a war, the same two gangs may not declare another for 24 hours.

5.6.6 Non-members, bystanders, business staff and EMS may not be targeted. Police may intervene normally. Do not shoot police unless they attack first or you are committing a crime under Section 6.

5.6.7 You may not start a war to avoid an arrest or a penalty, or to cancel a heist cooldown.

5.7 Guns

5.7.1 Guns are sorted into Class 1 (legal pistols from the shop), Class 2 (illegal pool from the arms dealer) and Class 3 (everything stronger, including all shotguns, player-made only).

5.7.2 Carrying a visible gun in a public place needs a reason. Drawing a gun in a safe zone is prohibited (Rule 8.4).

5.7.3 Gunsmith-made guns carry a serial and a maker's mark. Police can trace them in the MDT. You may not change, remove or hide a serial except by the in-game method, if one is added.

5.7.4 You may not craft, sell or trade guns through alts or for real money. You may not mass-supply weapons to people you know are about to commit a rule break (for example, selling 20 guns to a new account that then starts an RDM run).

5.7.5 The arms dealer moves between locations. Do not share his location publicly (street, Discord, stream) outside the characters who learned it IC.

5.8 House burglary

5.8.1 You may only rob the designated burglary houses. You may never rob a player home, an apartment, a player business or a hideout.

5.8.2 You may not wait outside a designated house to rob another burglar. A burglar's loaded car may be robbed only under Rule 6.5.

5.8.3 One crew per house at a time. You may not block entry or camp a house.

5.8.4 A burglar must carry items one by one by hand into a vehicle. You may not use exploits to move items faster (clipping, double-carrying, vehicle glitches).

5.8.5

5.8.6 High-value houses need at least 1 officer on duty. The server checks this when the job starts.

5.9 Grey market

5.9.1 Grey goods are fake or bootleg items under fictional brands. You may not use real-world brand names or logos in item names, stall signs or roleplay claims that imply a real brand.

5.9.2 A stall pitch is claimed on the tablet or by placing the stall kit. You may not block a pitch by parking or standing in it for longer than 10 minutes without selling.

5.9.3 Police may issue a citation and take the stock. You must comply with an IC citation and may not dispute it by hiding goods or disconnecting (Rule 3.7).

5.9.4 Flea market nights follow the posted schedule. No selling stolen goods from a house burglary at the flea market stalls other than through the fence NPC.

5.10 Heists and cop count

5.10.1 Heists have tiers that need on-duty officers (see the table). You may not start a heist without the officers the tier needs.

Tier Officers needed on duty
Small 0 to 1
Mid 2 or more
Big 3 or more

5.10.2 Cop count gaming is prohibited. You may not ask, pay, trick or force an officer to go off duty, switch to another character, or leave the city to change the count. You may not create a police alt or tell friends to go on duty to unlock a tier. Both sides are penalized.

5.10.3 The server counts officers when the heist starts. You may not start a heist, then ask officers to leave.

5.10.4 One heist at a time per gang or crew. Splitting your group to run two crimes at once is prohibited.

5.10.5 Cooldowns and global timers are set by the server. You may not bypass them with alts, new groups or script tricks.

5.10.6 No heist may start in the 30 minutes before a scheduled restart. A heist already running at that point may finish. No other illegal activity may start in the final 10 minutes before a restart.

5.10.7 Police need not wait for a heist to finish to start negotiating. See Section 6 for hostages.

5.11 Evidence transport

5.11.1 Police start a transport. It contains real seized items. Gangs may rob it as a heist (Section 6).

5.11.2 The notice that gangs receive is vague and delayed. You may not use out-of-character information (a police friend, a Discord leak, a stream) to find the route (Rule 3.3).

5.11.3 A police character who gives a gang the route is committing a violation, unless staff have approved the arc in advance.

5.12 Staff-run testing

5.12.1 Staff may use a test mode that simulates officer counts. It is not available to players. Using an exploit to copy it is a Tier 4 violation (Section 9).

06Robbery, Hostages and Police Contact (Civilian Side)

Police and EMS duty rules (SOPs) are written separately by the owner. This section covers what civilians and criminals must do.

6.1 Robbing players

6.1.1 You may rob a player only with a weapon pointed at them (or a clear and visible threat), a valid reason (Rule 4.1.1), and a roleplayed demand of at least 10 seconds.

6.1.2 You may take cash, dirty cash, drugs, guns, burglary loot and goods from their stall. You may not take their phone, ID, keys to a vehicle they legally own, clothing they are wearing, or items that the server marks as untakeable.

6.1.3 Maximum 1 robbery of the same person in 2 hours. Maximum 1 robbery of the same group in 1 hour.

6.1.4 You may not rob a person who is cuffed or ziptied unless you ziptied them as part of a valid robbery in the same scene. You may not rob a person who is downed or dead.

6.1.5 You may not rob a player who is in the first 30 minutes of a new character (Rule 4.6.2), at a job in progress, in a safe zone, or in a hospital or police station.

6.1.6 Stripping a victim (taking everything) is not allowed. Leave the victim with their phone, ID, keys and enough to roleplay (no less than 10 percent of their cash and one item).

6.2 Groups in a robbery or heist

6.2.1 Maximum group sizes for crimes are in the table below.

Crime Max robbers Max hostages
Player robbery, ATM, store 3 1
House burglary 4 none required
Small heist 4 2
Mid heist 6 2
Big heist 8 3

6.2.2 People from different groups working together in one crime must form a named group with matching outfits and register on the tablet as a crew before it starts.

6.2.3 Looking out for a crime from nearby (lookout, driver) counts toward the limit.

6.2.4 You may not add members during a scene. Replacements are allowed only for a member who has died, been arrested or left the scene, and the group cannot exceed the limit.

6.3 Tools and force in heists

6.3.1 Heists may use explosives only if the heist script provides them. Do not use extra explosives to break into other buildings.

6.3.2 You may not leave the scene to resupply weapons, armor or ammo, then return to the fight. Leaving means you cannot rejoin the same scene.

6.3.3 No nitrous in a getaway from a heist or robbery.

6.3.4 No AI medic or EMS call during a heist or robbery. A medical request can be a hostage demand (Rule 6.7.3).

6.4 Hostages

6.4.1 A hostage must be a stranger. Friends, gang members, crew members, alts and anyone who agreed OOC may not be a hostage.

6.4.2 You may not take a hostage in a crime that does not allow one in the table. In a small robbery, taking a hostage is a violation unless it is an escape from a failed robbery with police on scene.

6.4.3 Once police arrive, you may not swap or add hostages. If a hostage is freed, the robbers may not take a new hostage.

6.4.4 You may hold a hostage for no more than 30 minutes of real time. After that, negotiate or release.

6.4.5 A hostage must be treated as a person: no sudden killing, no torture, no repeated abuse. Do not threaten with CK.

6.4.6 A hostage player must roleplay fear and follow reasonable commands. You may not run, disconnect, use a phone or a radio, or call for help unless the robbers allow it.

6.4.7 Police officers may be hostages only in a scene staff approved or when the officer was captured in a firefight. EMS and patients may never be hostages.

6.5 Robbing the robbers

6.5.1 You may follow and rob a crew after a heist or burglary for their loot, using Rule 6.1 and a valid reason. The rob must be roleplayed first and not be a surprise shooting.

6.5.2 You may not rob a crew who are surrendering to police or in custody.

6.5.3 You may not wait outside a heist location to ambush the crew leaving.

6.6 Police contact (civilians and criminals)

6.6.1 When police give a lawful order (stop, hands up, get out), you must respond within 5 seconds in roleplay. You may comply, flee once, or negotiate. You may not ignore it.

6.6.2 Fleeing. You may flee on foot or by vehicle once per scene. If caught, you must stop and comply. You may not flee a second time in the same scene (a scene ends per Section 1.3).

6.6.3 Resisting. You may not shoot at an officer who is only arresting you unless you are committing a crime under this section. Shooting an officer in a routine stop is RDM.

6.6.4 Search and seizure. You must comply with a search. You may not hide items by dropping them, giving them to others, or using vehicles, stashes or your phone to avoid a search that officers have started.

6.6.5 Cuffs and zipties. A cuffed person cannot use items, weapons or phone. You may not break out of cuffs with an emote or a trick the game does not allow.

6.6.6 Do not insult police for no story reason. Do not abuse reports of officer actions (use Rule 9.4).

6.6.7 Prison and jail time are decided by police and the courts. Crime scripts never jail you. You must serve your sentence.

6.7 Negotiation

6.7.1 Demands must be believable. No demands for the whole server's cash, a plane, a tank, or the release of unrelated people.

6.7.2 You may ask for: a getaway vehicle, safe passage, cash within a limit staff set (default 100,000 total), or the release of one named person arrested in the same scene.

6.7.3 You may ask for medical attention for one person only, and only when there is a real medical need roleplayed.

6.7.4 If police have not responded to a demand after 10 minutes, they may breach. If they have made contact, they may not breach for 15 minutes, unless a hostage is hurt.

6.7.5 Police may not breach before the robbers' demands are heard, unless a hostage is hurt or the robbers open fire.

6.8 Scene conduct

6.8.1 Once the robbers and hostages have arrived, the scene is closed to outside players. Outsiders may not join, rescue or disrupt unless they were involved before it began.

6.8.2 Everyone must stay in scene until it is over. You may not be a bystander who watches from a safe place and gives the police information IC that you learned OOC.

6.8.3 If you crash out of the city, tell staff and rejoin the scene as it was (Rule 3.7.2).

07Economy, Businesses, Property, Exploits and Cheating

7.1 Cheating

7.1.1 No cheat software, mod menus, injectors, executors, macros, auto-clickers, aimbots, wallhacks, ESP, speed hacks, teleport, noclip or any program that gives an advantage the game does not.

7.1.2 No client modifications that change what the server shows or how the game plays (visual packs that remove foliage, walls or lighting for an advantage, or that hide player names or blips). Cosmetic graphics packs that do not give an advantage are allowed. If staff ask you to remove a pack, you must.

7.1.3 Staff may ask you to share a clip or your screen in Discord to check for cheats (Rule 9.6.5). Refusing a cheat check can be treated as a Tier 4 violation.

7.1.4 Sharing, selling or helping others to cheat is treated as cheating.

7.2 Exploits and duplication

7.2.1 An exploit is using a bug, glitch or a script mistake to gain money, items, XP, standing, influence, speed or safety that the system did not mean to give.

7.2.2 Duplication of any item, vehicle, cash or weapon is a permanent ban offence.

7.2.3 Report within 24 hours. If you find a bug, stop using it and report it to staff within 24 hours of finding it. Reports made in time earn a reward decided by staff.

7.2.4 If you used an exploit before you knew it was one, tell staff as soon as you learn. You must give back everything gained by it.

7.2.5 Using an exploit to gain an advantage in a scene (for example, clipping into a hideout, shooting through walls, or hiding in the map) is a violation even if no item was gained.

7.2.6 Staff may roll back, remove or correct anything gained through an exploit, with or without notice.

7.3 Economy conduct

7.3.1 All money, items and vehicles in the city belong to your character. You may not trade them for anything outside the server (real money, other games, accounts, gift cards).

7.3.2 Staff may adjust balances, prices and rewards for fairness. If something was paid or priced by mistake, you must report it (Rule 7.2.3).

7.3.3 You may not use alts, hidden accounts or gifts to shift money, items or standing between characters (Rule 2.7.1).

7.3.4 You may not stockpile to cause a shortage on purpose, or fix a price with the aim of stopping other players from playing their jobs. Pricing your own goods is allowed.

7.3.5 No AFK money. Any job, farm, plot, lab or process that earns money must be done with you present and attentive (Rule 5.4.4).

7.4 Scams and deals

7.4.1 You may scam another character in roleplay for in-game items and cash. The scam must be roleplayed, must be believable and must not use a game bug.

7.4.2 You may not scam for more than 25 percent of the other player's total cash, or for anything the player needs to keep playing (home, their only vehicle, job license), unless they agreed.

7.4.3 You may not scam for real money, real items or accounts. That is a permanent ban.

7.4.4 A deal made in roleplay in front of witnesses stands. A deal that was made OOC (Discord, text outside the city) does not count.

7.5 Businesses

7.5.1 Player businesses are bought only through the official store and created, assigned and removed only by staff. They cannot be sold, gifted, traded, lent or transferred by any method.

7.5.2 If you leave the server or are removed, the business goes back to staff.

7.5.3 The owner is responsible for what happens in the business, including illegal activity on the books. An owner who allows illegal activity must keep accurate records and give police access to audits.

7.5.4 Do not use a business to launder money unless the owner has switched on dirty deposits (Rule 5.3.4).

7.5.5 Do not close a business only to shut out a rival or deny a service for a week or longer without telling staff.

7.5.6 The owner may not make employees act against server rules, including to take a loss for tax or cover a money trail.

7.6 Homes, apartments and storage

7.6.1 Homes are given by staff only. They cannot be sold, traded or lent.

7.6.2 Player homes, apartments and hideouts cannot be robbed (Rule 5.8.1). Do not enter another player's home without an invitation.

7.6.3 Do not use storage as a way to avoid a legal search by police that has started (Rule 6.6.4). Police may search a home only through an MDT case and warrant.

7.6.4 Do not store illegal items in public lockers or stashes that other characters can open.

7.7 Supporter perks

7.7.1 Supporter items are cosmetic, convenience or access only. They do not give an advantage in conflict, income or the law.

7.7.2 Supporters follow every rule in this book. A supporter perk is never a defense.

7.7.3 Chargebacks, refund abuse or fraud get a permanent ban of the account and linked accounts.

7.7.4 No in-game money, items, or characters are sold for real money. Only the official store sells perks, and it never sells in-game currency.

08Vehicles, Driving and Zones

8.1 Driving

8.1.1 Drive like a person who owns the car and values it. No stunts, jumps or drifting in traffic. Racing is allowed only at approved events and designated spots.

8.1.2 Fail driving. Driving at high speed through traffic for no reason, without a story, or crashing and driving on, is Fail RP (Rule 3.1).

8.1.3 Seat belts, lights and indicators are optional. Crash damage and injuries follow the game systems.

8.1.4 You may not park or leave a vehicle in a way that blocks a door, a road, an entrance or a stall pitch for more than 5 minutes. Staff may move it.

8.1.5 Vehicle theft. You may steal a vehicle only with a valid reason and by the game's methods (lockpick, hotwire). You may not steal a vehicle that is parked at a player's home, garage or business. You may not steal a vehicle that a player is currently in unless it is a carjacking under Rule 6.1.

8.1.6 Vehicles you steal must be dumped or sold IC within 2 hours. You may not store them in a hideout or home.

8.1.7 Real brand names are not used on any vehicle, plate or shop sign that the server provides.

8.2 Vehicles as weapons

8.2.1 Ramming a person on foot is VDM unless it is part of a valid reason (Rule 4.1.3).

8.2.2 Using a vehicle to block a road to commit a crime is allowed only in a heist that allows it. It must be moved within 10 minutes of the end.

8.2.3 Using a car to get inside an interior, shop or building is prohibited.

8.3 Pursuits

8.3.1 A pursuit starts when an officer turns on lights or sirens and orders you to stop, or when you commit a crime in front of an officer and drive away.

8.3.2 You may flee once per scene (Rule 6.6.2). A pursuit must make sense: do not drive off a cliff, into water or onto rooftops, or take paths that vehicles cannot use.

8.3.3 No leaving the road network for more than 60 seconds. No using the shoreline, rail tracks or construction yards to lose the police.

8.3.4 Ramming police is allowed only in a pursuit and only to push the car (a PIT or a block). It is not a way to kill officers.

8.3.5 If you lose the police, you must go to a normal place and stop acting like a fugitive for at least 10 minutes.

8.3.6 You may not drive a stolen or wanted vehicle through a safe zone.

8.4 Safe zones

8.4.1 Safe zones are marked on the map. They include the spawn area, hospitals, police stations, the courthouse, and any other zone staff publish.

8.4.2 In a safe zone you may not: draw a weapon, attack, rob, kidnap, sell drugs, spray tags, run a stall pitch outside a flea market, or start any scene.

8.4.3 A crime that starts outside a safe zone and ends in one may continue only if the people involved leave the zone at once. They may not use the zone to heal, reset or hide.

8.4.4 Camping a safe zone entrance to wait for someone is prohibited.

8.5 Zones in the city

8.5.1 Gang blocks. Turf blocks are marked on the map. Entering a block triggers a one-line notice for non-police characters. The notice is information only. It does not allow violence.

8.5.2 Restricted places. Private interiors (police stations, hospitals, the courthouse, hideouts, and homes) are restricted. Only invited characters may enter.

8.5.3 Events. Event areas are posted by staff. Different rules may apply and will be posted.

09Staff, Penalties, Reports and Appeals

9.1 Penalty tiers

Every rule in this book belongs to a tier. Staff pick the tier by the harm done, not by how the player feels about it. Staff may skip steps for serious harm, and may never go below the first step.

9.1.1 A violation in Tier 4 is permanent on the first offense. No warning.

9.1.2 Steps reset after 60 days with no violation in that tier.

9.1.3 Admin jail (30 to 60 minutes out of the city) may replace a written warning when the player is disrupting a scene right now.

9.1.4 Staff may also remove items, money, vehicles or standing gained by the violation (Rule 7.2.6).

9.2 Gang strikes

9.2.1 A serious rule break by a gang member is a strike on the gang, as well as on the player (Rule 5.2.4).

9.2.2 Three strikes in 30 days: the gang is suspended for 7 days (no turf, hideout or tablet gang tab). A fourth strike in 30 days: staff may disband the gang.

9.3 How to report

9.3.1 Open a ticket in the Discord within 24 hours of the event. Later reports may be refused.

9.3.2 A report must have: your character name, the other character or player, the time, what happened, and a clip. The clip must show the full event, from 60 seconds before it started (Rule 2.3.2).

9.3.3 Do not argue in the city. Do not start a second scene to settle a report.

9.3.4 Staff answer every report within 24 hours. If a report is not answered in 24 hours, ping the staff team once.

9.4 Standard of proof

9.4.1 Staff need a clip, a server log, or two staff-checked statements. One person's word against another's with no clip or log is a warning to both sides and a note on the file.

9.4.2 Staff must write down the rule number, the tier, the evidence and the decision for every penalty. The player can ask to see it.

9.5 Appeals

9.5.1 You may appeal any penalty once, in a ticket, within 14 days. Say which rule, what happened and what you want.

9.5.2 A staff member who did not give the penalty reviews it. If a Level 3 Admin gave it, a Level 2 or Level 1 reviews it.

9.5.3 Permanent bans may be appealed after 30 days. Tier 4 permanent bans for cheating, hate speech or doxxing are not appealable.

9.5.4 Lying in an appeal doubles the penalty (Rule 2.3.1).

9.6 Staff conduct

9.6.1 Staff follow every rule in this book in the city. Staff roles give no exemptions. Staff may not use their tools for their own gain.

9.6.2 Staff may not punish a player they are in conflict with. They must hand the case to another staff member.

9.6.3 Staff may not share private tickets, logs or player data (Rule 2.2.2).

9.6.4 Staff decisions in the city are followed at once (Rule 1.1.3). A staff member who is wrong is corrected through the appeal, not in the moment.

9.6.5 Staff may ask you to share a clip or your screen in Discord to check for cheats. Staff may never ask to control your computer (remote-access tools). You may refuse that and report it.
